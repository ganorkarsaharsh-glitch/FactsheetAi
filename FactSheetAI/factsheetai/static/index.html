MILESTONE 3 · 0:55–1:30
Explain Overlap Arithmetic
“For every ISIN held by both schemes, we calculate min(wAᵢ, wBᵢ) and sum those minima. This is shared portfolio equity weight, not statistical return correlation. Notice the mathematical identity: Σ (wAᵢ + wBᵢ − |wAᵢ − wBᵢ|) / 2 matches identically.”
MILESTONE 4 · 1:30–2:00
Concentration & Effective Stocks (HHI)
“Top-10 sums the 10 largest equity positions. Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) sums squared fractional NAV weights: Σ (wᵢ/100)². Its reciprocal 1/HHI gives the number of equally weighted stocks that would generate the identical portfolio concentration.”
MILESTONE 5 · 2:00–2:25
Statutory Mandate Floor (AMFI Top-100 Schedule)
“SEBI mandates that large-cap funds invest at least 80% in large-cap stocks. We test this using the effective half-yearly AMFI Top-100 list matching that disclosure month (30 June 2025 for Jul–Dec 2025). We never apply future market-cap classifications retrospectively.”
MILESTONE 6 · 2:25–2:45
Dual-Engine Validation & Look-Through Rupee Weighting
“We cross-validate our calculations across two independent execution paths: a vectorized Pandas merge and pure Python Decimal dictionaries. Changing investor rupee allocation in the Portfolio Lab recalculates underlying rupee look-through exposure in real time while pairwise overlap remains allocation-independent.”
