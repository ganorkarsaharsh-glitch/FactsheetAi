Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Transparent answers regarding methodology, source files, and mathematical implementations.

How does FactSheetAI calculate pairwise overlap? ▼
For any two funds A and B, overlap is computed as Σ min(wAᵢ, wBᵢ) over the common set of equity ISINs, where each w is the disclosed percentage of Net Asset Value (NAV). This represents the exact percentage points of duplicated equity capital. It is an accounting-level measure of portfolio duplication, not a statistical correlation coefficient.
Why are ICICI Prudential factsheet PDFs excluded from ISIN calculations? ▼
ICICI Prudential monthly factsheets archive reference PDFs rather than full spreadsheet disclosures. Crucially, these PDFs omit security ISIN codes and aggregate small holdings into generalized line items. Under our strict fail-closed governance policy, FactSheetAI refuses to guess ISINs via fuzzy text matching. ICICI files are archived for reference but excluded from ISIN-level analytics.
How does the system ensure the correct AMFI Top-100 schedule is applied? ▼
AMFI publishes half-yearly market capitalization classifications. FactSheetAI maps each disclosure month strictly to the list in effect for that period (e.g. 30 June 2025 schedule for July–Dec 2025 disclosures, and 31 December 2025 schedule for Jan–June 2026 disclosures). Future lists are never applied retrospectively.
Can FactSheetAI be integrated into proprietary risk platforms via REST API? ▼
Yes. FactSheetAI exposes high-performance FastAPI endpoints: /v1/catalog for corpus metadata, /v1/analysis for pairwise metrics, /v1/portfolio-analysis for multi-fund matrices, and /v1/export/excel for automated workbook compilation. All responses return comprehensive provenance objects.
How are missing metadata fields researched without hallucinations? ▼
When triggered by the user in the Excel export suite, FactSheetAI searches official AMC domains exclusively for specific numeric fields (e.g. fund manager name, category expense average). Every accepted value requires an authoritative official AMC citation with URL and evidence quote. Portfolio holdings and math are never generated by language models.
